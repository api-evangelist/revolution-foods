A resource for schools, districts, nonprofits, and community organizations serving children when school is not in session. Summer is one of the most operationally complex seasons for school nutrition teams. The federal requirements don’t change; the students who need meals don’t disappear, and yet staff, equipment, and infrastructure are often reduced or temporarily offline.
